The app polls Steam and updates your Slack status. Run a single replica only.

To look up your Steam ID (one-time setup):
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "slack-steam-status.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl "http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/id?username=<your-steam-name>"

Health check:
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/health

Manual status update:
  curl http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/ping
